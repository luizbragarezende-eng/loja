The product

Ready-to-print PDF kits in A4. A teacher buys one, prints it for the whole class and can use it the same day. Payment runs through Kiwify with Pix, card or boleto.

Who buys it

Teachers in Brazil, mainly of grades 1 to 5, who prepare their own materials outside class hours.

What it covers

Literacy, Portuguese, math, inclusion, school dates, classroom games, and practice tests in the style of SIMAVE, the state assessment of Minas Gerais.

When teachers look for it

Demand follows the Brazilian school calendar, so each date is a natural moment for a creator video showing a printed kit in use.

About the brand

An independent store run by one teacher in Minas Gerais, Brazil, at an early stage. The long-term goal is simple: materials for every grade and every subject, made by a teacher for teachers.
